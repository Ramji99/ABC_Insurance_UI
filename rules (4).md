# Skill: Tone & Voice Rules (`tone-and-voice.skill.md`)

## Description
Enforces copy generation rules reflecting ABC ERGO's brand persona for insurance applications.

## Core Tone Attributes
* **Stimulating:** Light, engaging, and friendly.
* **Relatable:** Speaks directly in everyday language.
* **Social & Conversational:** Clear, approachable, and human.
* **Uplifting & Confident:** Builds trust while reducing perceived risk.
* **Informative:** Simple demystification of complex coverage terms.

## Content Construction Rules
1. **Be Clear:** Bring the primary health insurance benefit upfront.
2. **Be Succinct:** Hook attention with a strong headline; clarify in the sub-head.
3. **Be Simple:** Avoid dense insurance jargon.
4. **Be Promising:** Reflect reliability and peace-of-mind vision without intrusive copy.
